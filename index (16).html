Growing Dreams Since 1985

For over 35 years, Farm Pépinière has been the trusted source for quality trees in the region. What started as a small family nursery has grown into one of the largest tree farms, offering over 200 varieties of trees.

Our passionate team of horticulturists ensures every tree is healthy, strong, and ready to thrive in your garden. We believe in sustainable practices and work to preserve our environment while helping you create your perfect outdoor space.
